# Apartment Maintenance Analysis & Simulation
Project Overview

This project uses a Monte Carlo simulation in Python to analyze apartment maintenance workloads, staffing requirements, and operational costs.

The simulation models unpredictable maintenance requests across 500 apartments to estimate labor demand, evaluate staffing capacity, and assess potential overtime expenses.

The goal is to use data-driven analysis to help property management companies make more informed workforce planning and cost management decisions.

Business Problem

Property management companies must maintain sufficient staffing levels to respond to maintenance requests while controlling labor expenses.

However, maintenance demand is uncertain and can fluctuate from week to week. Insufficient staffing may result in overtime costs and operational inefficiencies.

This project addresses the problem by simulating different maintenance demand scenarios and evaluating their potential impact on staffing requirements and operating costs.

Project Objectives

Simulate weekly maintenance requests across 500 apartments.
Estimate total maintenance labor hours.
Evaluate available workforce capacity.
Identify potential overtime requirements.
Analyze weekly staffing and operational costs.
Apply simulation results to support workforce planning decisions.

Business Applications

This analysis demonstrates how simulation techniques can help organizations:
Anticipate uncertain maintenance workloads.
Evaluate staffing capacity and resource allocation.
Understand the financial impact of overtime.
Make more informed operational planning decisions.
